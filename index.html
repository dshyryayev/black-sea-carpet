FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the number of rooms, the square footage, the condition of the carpet and any extra treatments such as pet odor work. We provide free, no-obligation quotes so you know the price before we begin.

How long does carpet cleaning take?

Most residential jobs take between one and three hours depending on the size of the home and the condition of the carpet. Larger or heavily soiled jobs can take longer.

How long does it take for carpets to dry?

Carpets are typically damp to the touch and dry within 4 to 8 hours. Airflow, humidity and carpet thickness all affect drying time. Opening windows or running fans helps speed it up.

Do you remove pet stains and odors?

We treat pet stains and odors with specialized products and extraction. Many stains improve dramatically or disappear, but results depend on how long the stain has been there and whether the underlay or subfloor is affected. We cannot guarantee that every stain will be fully removed.

Can you clean sofas and sectional couches?

Yes. We clean sofas, sectionals, chairs, cushions and most fabric furniture. We check the fabric type first and use an appropriate method.

Do you clean area rugs?

Yes. We clean area rugs on site using methods suited to the rug's material and condition. Some delicate or antique rugs may need specialized handling, and we'll let you know if that's the case.

Do you offer commercial carpet cleaning?

Yes. We work with offices, restaurants, rental properties, property managers and commercial buildings, including after-hours scheduling where needed.

Do you offer move-in and move-out carpet cleaning?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleaning is one of our most requested services for tenants, landlords and property managers.

Do I need to move my furniture before cleaning?

It helps if smaller items, breakables and floor clutter are moved beforehand. We can move light furniture where it's safe to do so. We don't move heavy items such as beds, large dressers or electronics.

How can I get a quote?

Call or text us, or fill out the free quote form on this page. Share a few details about the rooms, furniture and any problem areas and we'll get back to you with pricing.
